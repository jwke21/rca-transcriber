# Incident 555555: Login service 500 errors in US East 1 due to expired database connections

**Date:** 2026-10-06

**Author:** RCA Transcriber

## Summary

At 16:31, the login service went down, reporting 500 errors in US East 1. Logs indicated database query errors and showed a database failover to US West 2 occurred one minute prior to the failures. The engineer determined the likely root cause was expired connections in the US East 1 cluster's connection pool. To resolve the issue, the engineer refreshed the US East 1 login service cluster at 16:32, which successfully restored the service.
