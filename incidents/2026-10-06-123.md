# Incident 123: Login service 500 errors in US East 1 due to expired connections

**Date:** 2026-10-06

**Author:** RCA Transcriber

## Summary

At 19:07, the login service went down, with metrics showing 500 errors in the US East 1 region. Database logs indicated a failover had occurred one minute prior. The engineer restarted the US East 1 login service cluster at 19:07, which refreshed the service and restored activity. The likely root cause was identified as expired connections in the US East 1 connection pool.
