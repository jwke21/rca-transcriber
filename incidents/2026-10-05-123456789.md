# Incident 123456789: Login service 5xx errors in US East 1 after database failover

**Date:** 2026-10-05

**Author:** RCA Transcriber

## Summary

At 04:34, the login service went down, with metrics showing 5xx errors in US East 1. Logs revealed database query errors, and the engineer found that a database failover to US West 2 had occurred one minute before the failures began. At 04:35, the engineer refreshed the US East 1 login service cluster, successfully restoring the service. The likely cause was expired connections in the US East 1 cluster's connection pool.
