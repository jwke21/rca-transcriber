# Incident 123: Login service 500 errors in US East 1 due to database failover

**Date:** 2026-09-28

**Author:** RCA Transcriber

## Summary

At 17:55, the login service in US East 1 went down, with metrics reporting 500 errors and logs showing database query errors. The engineer checked database logs for US East 1 and US West 2, finding that a database failover to US West 2 had occurred one minute before the login service began reporting failures. At 17:56, the engineer refreshed the US East 1 cluster, which successfully restored the login service. The likely root cause was identified as expired connections in the US East 1 cluster's connection pool.
