# Incident 5555: Login service 500 errors in US East 1 due to expired database connections

**Date:** 2026-10-06

**Author:** RCA Transcriber

## Summary

At 16:19, the login service went down, with metrics reporting 500 errors in US East 1 and logs showing database query errors. The engineer checked database logs and found a database failover to US West 2 occurred one minute before the login service failures. To resolve the issue, the engineer refreshed the US East 1 login service cluster. The service became active again shortly after. The likely root cause was identified as expired connections in the US East 1 cluster's connection pool.
