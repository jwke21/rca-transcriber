# Incident 111111: Login service 500 errors in US East 1 due to database queue depth

**Date:** 2026-09-28

**Author:** RCA Transcriber

## Summary

At 05:00 PM on 09/27/2026, the login service in US East 1 began experiencing 500 errors. The on-call engineer investigated the logs and discovered that the errors occurred when the login service called the database. A check of the database dashboard revealed that the database queue depths had exceeded safe limits.

To resolve the issue, the engineer restarted the database. After a successful restart, a validation suite was run to confirm that the login service was functioning normally, and the incident was closed at 05:15 PM.
