# Incident 12: Login service down in US East 1 due to database failover

**Date:** 2026-09-28

**Author:** RCA Transcriber

## Summary

At 17:37, the login service was reported down with metrics showing 500 errors in US East 1. Database logs indicated that a database failover occurred one minute prior to the service failure. The engineer noted the error was likely caused by expired connections in the connection pool. To resolve the incident, the engineer refreshed the US East 1 cluster at 17:38. The login service was revalidated and confirmed to be working, with the incident resolved at 17:39.
